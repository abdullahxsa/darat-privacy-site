Google Play والمراسلات

قد يعالج Google Play بيانات الحساب والتثبيت والاختبار وتشخيصات المنصة وفق إعداداتك وسياسة Google. وإذا راسلت AppsVexa خارج اللعبة، يستلم المطوّر ما تختار إرساله، مثل بريدك الإلكتروني ورسالتك، للرد والمتابعة.

يمكنك حذف التقدم والنجوم والمحاولة والإعدادات بمسح بيانات التطبيق من إعدادات Android أو بإزالة التطبيق. يُحتفظ بالمراسلات للمدة اللازمة للرد والمتابعة، ويمكنك طلب حذفها عبر قناة التواصل التي استخدمتها.

Privacy policy

دارات | DARAT · AppsVexa

Effective date 2026-11-10

This policy explains how AppsVexa handles data for دارات | DARAT (com.darkcode.game).

Data stored on your device

The game saves completed stages, best move counts and stars, the stage ID and move history of an unfinished attempt, and your visual effects and haptic feedback preferences. It uses this data to unlock stages, show results, resume your attempt and apply your settings. It stays in the private app storage on your device. The app’s Android backup and transfer rules exclude its progress and settings files.

Transmission and sharing

The app does not transmit game data or preferences off your device or share them with AppsVexa or other parties. It has no accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics or built-in crash reporting. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Google Play and correspondence

Google Play may process account, installation, testing and platform diagnostics data according to your settings and Google’s policy. If you contact AppsVexa outside the game, the developer receives what you choose to send, such as your email address and message, to respond and follow up.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your progress, stars, saved attempt and settings by clearing app data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Correspondence is kept as long as needed to respond and follow up. You can request its deletion through the channel you used to contact us.
